A simulated banking pipeline that processes customer KYC (Know Your Customer) records and transaction data, modeled on the kind of validation and reporting workflows used in regulated financial environments.

Incoming KYC documents and transaction batches land in Amazon S3, where AWS Step Functions coordinates a series of Lambda and Glue (PySpark) jobs: validating required KYC fields, flagging incomplete or high-risk records, deduplicating transactions, and rolling everything up into curated tables used for regulatory-style reporting.

Why this project

Built to reflect real banking/fintech data engineering work — KYC pipelines and regulatory reporting — with an emphasis on data quality, auditability, and correctness over raw throughput.
