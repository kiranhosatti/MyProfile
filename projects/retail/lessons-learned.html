What I'd tell someone starting this project

  • Making every task idempotent (safe to re-run for the same logical date) turned backfills from a risky manual process into a routine DAG re-trigger.
  • A handful of stores had wildly skewed sales volume, which caused uneven Spark partition sizes; repartitioning by a combination of store and date fixed the straggler tasks.
  • Airflow SLAs and alerting caught a silently-stalled sensor early in development that would otherwise have delayed the whole day's pipeline.
  • Keeping data-quality checks as separate, visible DAG tasks (rather than buried inside the transform code) made failures much easier to diagnose.
